# Survival systems

| System | Current behaviour and links |
|---|---|
| Survivor and Sandbox | Advanced setup applies starting skills, equipment and trait multipliers. The rules govern infected, inventory perishability, utilities and the simulation clock. Equipment shows the selected traits and their effects. |
| Conditions | Hunger, thirst, sleep, exertion, panic, pain and exposure show graded conditions and affect stamina, movement, healing or combat. |
| Wounds | Arm and leg injuries bleed and can develop a local infection. Bandaging and disinfecting change the recovery path. Viral exposure progresses separately. |
| Clothing | Worn equipment reduces incoming damage and some wounds. Protection and insulation depend on condition. Rain and cold increase exposure outdoors. |
| Progression | Actions and skill books award XP. Skill levels feed into carrying, combat, treatment, construction, cooking, foraging and fishing. |
| Combat | Melee, shove and pistol attacks trade stamina, range, noise and durability against immediate safety. |
| Infected | Visual detection, sound investigation, nests and hordes put pressure on noisy tasks and occupied shelters. |
| World | Seeded districts connect Cold Vale to rural, industrial and coastal areas. Doors, supplies, discovered areas and map notes persist across visits. |
| Sustenance | Perishable food, cooking, fishing, gardening and collected water provide alternatives to scavenging. |
| Shelter and utilities | Barricades slow attacks. Grid power can fail, and a fuelled generator restores local power while attracting infected. |
| Vehicles | Cars provide transport and storage. Collisions and fuel use can end a trip, and engine noise attracts nearby infected. |

The shared timestamp discrepancy in the firewatch recorder, substation freezer log, and Night Reel photo strip connects three existing finds across Cold Vale and the cinema. The film and TV references remain in the research notes and environmental clues.

## Verify the survivor setup

1. Open **Advanced**, choose Doctor, and confirm the preview shows First Aid Lv. 3 and medical supplies.
2. Open **Appearance and traits**. Select Light Eater to see the starting button disable. Select Needs More Sleep to restore the budget and choose a raincoat.
3. Open **Customize**, change life of perishables to 50%, set the seed to 17, and start.
4. Open Gear to confirm the raincoat and both traits. Open Health to verify the starting skill.
5. Reload and choose **Continue**. Verify the same selection in Gear and confirm your world did not reset.
