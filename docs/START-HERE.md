# Last CloneZ quick start

Choose **Recommended start**. In the shelter, take the wooden bat, then open **Inventory** and equip it. Check a door before leaving. Look for supplies in nearby houses and return to the shelter before it gets dark.

| Action | Keyboard and mouse | Phone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move | WASD, arrow keys, or click the ground | Drag the movement stick or tap the ground |
| Run | Hold Shift while moving | Tap Run, then move |
| Interact | Press F or click the object | Tap Use or tap the object |
| Attack or shove | Space or X | Attack or Shove |
| Sneak | C | Sneak |
| Inventory, map, journal | I, M, J | Inventory, map, and journal buttons |
| Pause | Escape or P | Pause button |

Food, water, rest, and quiet keep you alive. Spend supplies on useful equipment and reserve enough to get home. **Health** shows skills grouped by activity. Practice comes from doing the work.

Read field notes when you find them and revisit them in **Journal → Field notes**. Inspect mushroom patches before picking. Moving filaments warn that harvesting may attract nearby infected. Identify the caps with Foraging level 2 or the Cold Vale Foraging Guide. Cook only identified Pantrycaps. Bitterfold is toxic.

At a campfire, recipes you have ingredients for appear first. Check the shelter crate and grocery store for oats, flour and beans. Look for dried ingredients at the orchard farmhouse, Net shed and district kitchens. Harvest fruit outdoors for porridge, compote and dried apple rings.

**Pause → Save and exit** returns to the title screen. Use **Continue** to resume. Export a save from the journal to keep a separate copy.
