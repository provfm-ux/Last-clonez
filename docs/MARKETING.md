# Last CloneZ demo: marketing kit (itch.io)

Fees, revenue shares and platform rules change. Check itch.io's current terms (revenue share, payouts) before you publish. Nothing here was verified live.

## 1. Pitch (use everywhere)
- **One line:** Two friends cloned a zombie game. Their AI made the virus real. Survive 30 nights in a free browser demo.
- **Hook for the demo:** *A cult cinema. A golden idol. 50 zombies when you grab it.* Play 30 minutes, free, no download.
- **Tone:** grim, a little darkly funny (the raffle, the beer tap, the Twinkie). Player-facing text stays English.

## 2. Story to tease (no big spoilers)
Safe to show: the radio rumour of a prepper fortress; Eva, who guides you and dreams of reaching it; five coordinates hidden in five areas; the cinema raffle that never finished; the Grotto in the dark.
Keep secret (use only as "there is a twist"): the preppers are the two friends; the AI boss; Pip as the last boss; Eva's fate on day 5.
Teaser lines: "Someone left clues across the region. Someone wants them found." · "The raffle stopped at number twelve." · "Every area has one challenge that changes how you play."

## 3. itch.io page
- **Title:** Last CloneZ (Demo) · **Short text (<100 chars):** Isometric zombie survival in your browser. Cult cinema, golden idol, 30 minutes.
- **Kind:** HTML, play in browser. **Genre:** Survival. **Tags (pick ~10):** zombies, survival, horror, isometric, pixel-art, singleplayer, browser, atmospheric, demo, 2d.
- **Cover image 630x500:** the cinema hall with the idol and the horde behind it; big readable logo. Make it readable at thumbnail size.
- **Screenshots (4-6):** 1 cinema foyer, 2 idol moment, 3 horde, 4 grotto in flashlight light, 5 inventory, 6 map. Add 1 short GIF or video (15-30 s) as the first media item.
- **Description layout:** hook line, 3 bullets (what you do), a 15 s GIF, controls, "this demo is 30 minutes", how to report bugs (the in-game button), what the full game adds (section 2), follow link.
- **Community:** turn on comments or a devlog; reply to every comment in week 1.
- **Pricing:** free demo. Decide the full game price and platform later; start the wishlist/follow habit now.

## 4. Short-video plan (TikTok / Reels / Shorts, same clip on all three)
Post 3-5 clips a week for 4 weeks. Movement in the first second, text hook, payoff first.
1. **Idol grab:** hand takes idol, CLICK, doors slam, horde pours out. Text: "Never take the golden idol."
2. **Escape:** the window escape with a horde behind you. Text: "Cinema rule 1: use the window."
3. **Grotto:** flashlight dies in the dark. Text: "My battery lasted 40 seconds."
4. **Funny:** the members' beer tap / the BUS FARES envelope. Text: "The raffle proceeds."
5. **Devlog:** "I made a zombie game in vanilla JS, no engine" (build breakdown, 1 min).
Always end with "Free demo, link in bio" and put the itch link in bio.

## 5. Where to post (free)
- itch.io devlogs and the #devlog tag; r/WebGames, r/indiegames, r/playmygame (read each rule first; many allow one self-promo post, with a clear label and a real answer to comments).
- TikTok/Reels/Shorts as above; Mastodon/Bluesky/X with #indiedev #screenshotsaturday (Saturdays).
- Small streamers and YouTubers: send a short personal note and the link, not a mass message; offer to fix what they find.
- Game jams on itch.io: a themed jam entry brings players; a finished demo can be re-released as a jam build.

## 6. Tips
1. **Ship the demo early, improve weekly.** Post an itch devlog with each update; the algorithm and followers reward activity.
2. **Make the first 60 seconds count:** the demo starts in the cinema with the lights on the idol; check it on a phone and a low-end laptop.
3. **Measure:** itch shows views, plays and downloads; the in-game report button gives bug text. Count how many players reach the 30-minute end screen (ask in comments).
4. **Ask for one thing:** at the end screen the offer is follow + share + report bugs; do not stack five asks.
5. **Collect feedback in public:** pin a feedback thread, thank people by name, fix the top 3 issues and post "you said, we fixed".
6. **Add a mailing list or Discord only if you will use it.** A follow on itch is enough to start.
7. **Do not spend on ads yet.** Spend time on clips and replies. Re-check your numbers after 2-3 weeks.
8. **Keep the demo browser-safe:** test it in the itch embed on Chrome, Firefox and mobile Safari (audio starts after the first click).
9. **Accessibility and comfort:** keep the flicker/atmosphere options reachable; screenshots with good contrast sell better.

## 7. Build with your links
`DEMO_EMAIL=bugs@you.com DEMO_URL=https://yourname.itch.io/last-clonez npm run build:demo`
`DEMO_URL` adds the Follow and Share buttons to the end screen.
